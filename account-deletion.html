  1. Open the 운명키우기 app.
  2. Go to the [내정보 (My info)] tab and tap [회원 탈퇴 (Delete account)] at the bottom. In guest mode the button is [이 기기의 기록 모두 지우기 (Delete all records on this device)].

Account data and saju records are stored only on your phone, so uninstalling the App also deletes them.
